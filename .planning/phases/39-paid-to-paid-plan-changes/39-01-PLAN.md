Purpose: Give support and any future billing system (BILL-01) a queryable trail of what a brokerage was billed at each paid-to-paid transition (D-01), reusing the existing async dispatch point (D-02), seat-counting rule (D-03), and centralized pricing config (D-04) rather than inventing new paths.

Output: One migration + one non-paranoid model + one factory, a `Brokerage#billable_seat_count` accessor, the filled-in `recalculate_billing!` job branch, and extended job tests covering the happy path and overage edge cases.
</objective>
